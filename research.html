When Knowledge-Tracing Rankings Depend on Available History

ASSISTments 2009 and 2015: source correction and matched-information evaluation

Question
Do source corrections and available learner history change the interpretation of knowledge-tracing model rankings?
Method
Data-provenance audit; learner-level held-out evaluation with matched preceding interaction histories; Brier-score comparison.
Evidence
817 of 4,148 learner sequences differed across source versions. On 20,592 held-out responses, order-aware logistic regression scored 0.17389 versus 0.17514 for a GRU with up to 20 preceding events.
Boundary
The comparison does not isolate an effect of model architecture.

Working manuscript2026arXiv submission in preparation

Early Assessment Submission and Subsequent Course Outcome

OULAD: a day-30 landmark observational analysis with weighting diagnostics

Question
How is assessment submission by day 30 associated with subsequent outcomes among eligible learners?
Method
Day-30 landmark; inverse-probability and overlap weighting; bootstrap intervals, balance and effective-sample-size checks.
Evidence
1,820 learners analyzed. Inverse-probability-weighted difference in favorable outcomes: 0.386 (95% bootstrap interval 0.324 to 0.445); overlap-weighted difference: 0.326.
Boundary
Large weights, remaining imbalance and an effective sample size of about 510 prevent a causal interpretation.
Methods and resultsCode and manuscript

Wrong-Answer-Conditioned Retrieval

Question
Does using a wrong-answer option improve retrieval of supporting educational evidence?
Evaluation
884 questions and 2,652 proxy cases with a shuffled-answer control. BM25 MRR: 0.9472 for question only versus 0.9433 with wrong-answer expansion.
Boundary
No distinct retrieval advantage was established for wrong-answer expansion.

Classroom Discourse Classification

Question
Can teacher discourse moves be classified while preventing duplicate transcripts from leaking across data splits?
Evaluation
175,129 utterances in 565 transcript groups. On 113 held-out groups, logistic regression macro-F1 was 0.5198 versus a majority baseline of 0.1152.

B.Eng. Thesis · Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB) · 2010

Image Retrieval System Using Geotagged Information

  • Developed and evaluated an image retrieval system combining textual information with geographic metadata stored in JPEG EXIF fields.
  • Used the vector space model and compared TF, IDF, and TF-IDF weighting schemes; evaluated retrieval using precision, recall, and Non-Interpolated Average Precision.
  • Demonstrated that geotagging could be integrated with text-based retrieval to support location-aware image search.
Undergraduate Technical Paper · Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB) · 2005–2006 academic year

Penerapan Algoritma Greedy pada Optimasi Pengaturan Lampu Lalu Lintas Sederhana

[Application of a Greedy Algorithm to Optimize Simple Traffic Light Control]

  • Coauthored with Rocky Hartono and Joel THP Hutasoit a proposed greedy-algorithm approach to choosing green-light durations for a simplified four-road traffic intersection.
  • Modeled incoming and outgoing vehicle counts, estimated queue densities and candidate signal timings to explore more balanced traffic flow.
  • The paper presents an analytical model and proposed algorithm, not a validated real-world traffic deployment; its conclusions acknowledge that greedy selection does not always provide a globally optimal solution.
Output: Hartono, R., Saputra, D. W., & Hutasoit, J. T. H. Penerapan Algoritma Greedy pada Optimasi Pengaturan Lampu Lalu Lintas Sederhana. Undergraduate technical paper, Department of Informatics Engineering, Institut Teknologi Bandung (2005–2006 course archive).
