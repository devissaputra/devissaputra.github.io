Portfolio in AI in Education

My work in Artificial Intelligence in Education combines an established background in learning design with developing capability in machine learning and data analysis. The projects cover learner modeling, assessment, retrieval, feedback and responsible use of AI, with particular attention to teacher judgment and learner agency. Empirical studies are separated from research prototypes, and each entry states what the available data and evaluation can support.

Empirical Studies

These four empirical studies examine predictive, observational, retrieval, and audit questions using external datasets. The linked calculation guides distinguish recorded findings from educational and causal claims.

Empirical Study · AI in Education

Knowledge Tracing Benchmark on ASSISTments 2009

The knowledge tracing benchmark measures how much predictive value is added by different representations of learner history. It contains 4,148 ASSISTments 2009 learners and 274,331 interactions, with learners separated into 70% training, 15% validation and 15% final test partitions. One fixed protocol compares a global prior, a skill prior, fixed and validation tuned Bayesian Knowledge Tracing, a PFA style logistic model based on earlier learner skill successes and failures, and a compact GRU sequence model. Every stateful prediction is produced before the current response is observed, preventing leakage of the target response into the model state.

On 623 held out learners, the seed 42 GRU reaches ROC AUC 0.7470 and Brier score 0.1800, compared with 0.6982 and 0.1976 for PFA. Across three GRU seeds, mean ROC AUC is 0.7471 with a sample standard deviation of 0.0008. The result supports improved response prediction on this historical benchmark. It does not establish instructional benefit or justify high stakes interpretation of an individual learner's knowledge.

Empirical Study · AI in Education

Causal Learning Analytics, OULAD Day 30 Landmark Study

The OULAD analysis addresses a narrow observational question: among learners still eligible at day 30, what adjusted difference in favorable final outcome is associated with submitting at least one non banked assessment by that point? Eligibility, exposure and later outcome timing are defined separately so that early withdrawal is not treated as if it were treatment assignment.

The recorded Hájek contrast is 0.3861, with a full refit bootstrap interval of approximately 0.3242 to 0.4447. The analysis also identifies extreme weights, a limited effective sample size and residual imbalance. An overlap weighted sensitivity analysis gives 0.3261 for a different target population. These are observational contrasts. Unmeasured confounding and selection remain unresolved, so the evidence does not establish that requiring early submission would improve outcomes.

Data source: Open University Learning Analytics Dataset (OULAD), using a frozen day 30 landmark cohort.

Empirical Study · AI in Education

Misconception Aware RAG Tutor, Empirical Study

The retrieval analysis is kept separate from the tutoring prototype built around it. On SciQ, the experiment tests whether adding an observed wrong answer to the query changes retrieval of supporting passages. A negative control appends shuffled distractor text so that any change can be compared with the effect of adding unrelated information.

Expansion with the wrong answer slightly reduces MRR from 0.9472 to 0.9433 in the fixed benchmark. The interval for the contrast with the shuffled distractor control spans zero, so the experiment does not establish a distinct benefit from the semantic relationship of the wrong answer. The measured retrieval result is therefore reported separately from the tutoring concept, with no claim of improved learning.

Data source: SciQ test split from the Allen Institute for AI / Hugging Face dataset repository, pinned to a frozen revision and hash.

Empirical Study · AI in Education

Responsible AIED Evaluation, UCI 697 Study

The responsible AIED evaluation audits an enrollment time dropout probability model across discrimination, calibration, subgroup error patterns, allocation behavior, target definition and split sensitivity. Protected group fields are used only for audit, while semester performance variables are excluded to preserve the intended prediction time boundary. The results are presented as model measurement rather than as a certificate of fairness.

The primary holdout gives ROC AUC 0.8316 and Brier score 0.1447. Gender diagnostics can be computed, while international, intersectional and special needs audits remain unevaluable under the stated support rules. Repeated splits and bootstrap analyses describe uncertainty, but incomplete governance evidence and the historical nature of the dataset prevent a deployment or fairness certification.

Data source: UCI Predict Students' Dropout and Academic Success (dataset 697), DOI 10.24432/C5MC89.

GenAI Learning Observatory

GenAI Learning Observatory converts synthetic AI interaction logs into session summaries of verification, revision, reflection and answer adoption. Intent labels are defined by explicit lexical rules, and the agency index is a weighted heuristic rather than a psychological measure. The implementation is useful for testing instrumentation and analysis procedures before collecting consented learner data.

Teacher and AI Assessment Studio

Teacher AI Assessment Studio compares synthetic human and AI assessment scores and creates a review queue from explicit disagreement and uncertainty thresholds. It reports exact agreement, agreement within one score point, quadratic kappa and the proportion routed for review. These outputs test routing behavior; they do not establish the correctness of an AI assessor or validate the thresholds for real students.

Privacy Preserving Learning Analytics

Privacy Preserving Learning Analytics demonstrates federated learning with synthetic learner records stored in local shards. Clients share clipped gradients, optionally with added noise, while a separate synthetic test set measures the effect on predictive utility. The implementation exposes the update rule and client weighting. Formal privacy accounting, secure aggregation and adversarial evaluation remain outside the current scope.

Multimodal Self Regulation Lab

Multimodal Self Regulation Lab compares synthetic interaction, attention proxy and self report signals using one common held out partition. The classifier pipeline fits imputation and scaling only on training rows, correcting an earlier evaluation design that reused information from the full sample. A separate rank fusion comparator explores reliability weighting. Because both the signals and outcomes are simulated, the results do not validate measurement of self regulation or attention in real learners.

Learning Design Process Mining

Learning Design Process Mining reconstructs synthetic instructional design event traces and reports common process variants, stage transition shares, entropy and a defined repetition rate. The measures make workflow patterns visible without treating every revision as inefficiency. Meaningful conclusions would require authentic design logs and a defensible interpretation of the stage labels.

Hybrid Intelligence Lab

Hybrid Intelligence Lab compares human decisions, AI decisions and confidence routed combinations while retaining the routing reason for every case. The synthetic generator deliberately links confidence with correctness, so strong combined performance partly reflects that assumption. Threshold sweeps show how often the policy defaults to human judgment and under which simulated conditions complementary errors can be exploited.

Explanation Faithfulness for AIED

Explanation Faithfulness for AIED computes explanation diagnostics from probability changes and feature overlap on synthetic cases. Comprehensiveness, sufficiency gap and stability are reported separately, while the combined index is identified as a weighted heuristic. The current software accepts supplied perturbation outputs. A substantive empirical evaluation would require actual model interventions and suitable random feature controls.

Collaborative Reasoning Analytics

Collaborative Reasoning Analytics combines explicit dialogue move labels, lexical overlap between adjacent turns and speaker participation balance on synthetic conversations. These quantities can support inspection of a coding procedure, but shared vocabulary does not establish conceptual uptake and balanced participation does not establish equitable reasoning. Human annotation and validation are therefore required before substantive interpretation.

Cognitive Offloading Analytics

Cognitive Offloading Analytics combines copying similarity, revision, verification, delayed recall and confidence change into an explicit offloading proxy. The chosen weights are visible and alternative weighting scenarios are tested. Scores are normalized within each cohort and the bands are heuristic, so the results support sensitivity analysis rather than diagnosis of individual dependence on AI.

Classroom Discourse Intelligence

Classroom Discourse Intelligence evaluates teacher talk move classification on the TalkMoves corpus. A majority baseline is compared with logistic regression using TF IDF features after duplicate transcript copies are removed and matching transcript text is grouped before the split. The final dataset contains 565 transcript groups and 175,129 labeled teacher utterances. On 113 held out groups, logistic regression reaches macro F1 0.5198 compared with 0.1152 for the majority baseline, while accuracy remains similar. The result concerns coded discourse moves and does not measure teaching quality.

Adaptive Socratic Tutor

Adaptive Socratic Tutor applies a transparent support policy to supplied mastery estimates and attempt counts. The rules move from a focused question toward a worked substep while keeping the complete answer outside the main response policy. Synthetic cases make the decision boundaries inspectable, but the package neither estimates mastery itself nor demonstrates an effect on learning.

Instructor Insight Engine

Instructor Insight Engine converts synthetic activity events into learner summaries and simple flags for instructor review. Accuracy is calculated from aggregated correct and attempted responses, and the low accuracy flag requires at least three attempts. Class level summaries are also reported. Learner State Sequence Model

Learner State Sequence Model summarizes supplied learner state sequences through transition probabilities, entropy of outgoing states and persistence. Probabilities are normalized only where transitions are observed, and insufficient sequences return an undefined persistence value rather than an invented estimate. The method is a descriptive sequence baseline; the state labels require independent justification and are not treated as inferred mental states.

Assessment Design Lab

Assessment Design Lab computes item difficulty, discrimination based on extreme groups and internal consistency diagnostics from synthetic response matrices. Rest scores exclude the focal item when comparison groups are formed, and undefined quantities remain explicit when the data cannot support an estimate. Flagged items are presented for review rather than treating alpha or a threshold as proof of assessment validity.

Learning Experiment Platform

Learning Experiment Platform stores assignment, baseline measures, follow up outcomes and attrition in one record. It reports group differences, effect sizes based on pooled standard deviation and bootstrap intervals while flagging sparse groups and baseline imbalance. Missing outcomes are handled as complete cases grouped by assignment, so the documentation limits causal interpretation and does not describe the analysis as a complete intention to treat estimate.

Learner Agency Simulator

Learner Agency Simulator compares support policies while allowing synthetic learners to request, accept or decline assistance. It records state occupancy, intervention burden, recovery and time to first mastery across seeded trajectories and sensitivity scenarios. Competency Gap Intelligence

Competency Gap Intelligence compares role requirements with dated evidence that carries an explicit confidence label, while keeping missing or stale evidence visible. Only gaps with sufficient support are ranked, after which prerequisite relationships shape development sequences and resource matches. Workplace Learning Recommender

Workplace Learning Recommender first filters resources for feasibility and then ranks them against confirmed competency gaps and work context. The score exposes contributions from need priority, gap coverage, task fit, quality, effort and other declared factors, followed by coverage and weight sensitivity checks. The output is a recommendation based on supplied metadata, not an estimate of learning impact.

Training Transfer Analytics separates workplace application from transfer conditions and business outcomes. Engagement Early Warning System

Engagement Early Warning System enforces a strict time boundary between predictors available at the point of assessment and outcomes observed later. Synthetic risk coefficients are exposed, and a capacity limited review queue is evaluated together with calibration and subgroup diagnostics. The code demonstrates leakage controls and support oriented reporting, but it does not claim predictive validity for real learners.

Curriculum Knowledge Graph

Curriculum Knowledge Graph represents courses, concepts, outcomes, assessments and resources through typed relationships with recorded provenance. It checks relation semantics and identifies prerequisite paths, cycles, disconnected entities and gaps in assessment coverage. These structural diagnostics support expert review of a curriculum; the graph does not independently verify the pedagogical correctness of its relationships.

Self Regulated Learning Copilot

Self Regulated Learning Copilot uses supplied plans, progress, preferences and interaction history to decide whether assistance should be offered or withheld. Refusals and explicit help requests are preserved, prompt burden is limited and reflection is connected to later strategy choices. The examples are synthetic, while plan completeness and struggle labels are operational rules rather than validated psychological measures.

Feedback Quality Evaluator

Feedback Quality Evaluator assesses feedback against supplied task context using separate rubric dimensions. Missing evidence remains unevaluable, lexical triggers are exposed and rater agreement tools handle degenerate cases explicitly. The implementation is a baseline for validation and error analysis rather than an automated authority on disciplinary correctness or feedback quality.
