Research Profile

My work connects Artificial Intelligence in Education, learning analytics, and learning systems design. I study learner modeling, knowledge tracing, data provenance, and the evaluation of AI-supported learning, with an emphasis on reproducible methods and careful interpretation of observational evidence. This research builds on my experience in university teaching, engineering management curriculum leadership, and Moodle-based workplace learning. I aim to examine how rigorous empirical research can inform the design and evaluation of learning technologies.
