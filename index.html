Research Profile

My work connects learning design, information systems and Artificial Intelligence in Education. I have led an engineering management program, taught undergraduate courses and developed a workplace learning system. My current independent research examines learner modeling, knowledge tracing, learning analytics, retrieval evaluation and the limits of observational educational data. The studies document data sources, evaluation methods and limitations, distinguishing predictive or associational findings from causal claims.
